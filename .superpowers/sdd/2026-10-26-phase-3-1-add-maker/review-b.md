### Important

**I1 — «انسخ بيانات الدخول للمبتكر» without `canLogin()` tells the maker a link will come even when his mode is `temp`** (Q3, Q7; Review Focus 9).
`28-app.js:1818-1819` (`copyLogin`) appends `'لينك التفعيل: هيوصله لما بوابة المبتكر تشتغل'` whenever it cannot fetch the password — i.e. for every member without `canLogin()` — regardless of `L0.pwMode`. The button itself is offered to everyone with a login row (`:1825`). Probe P6 (viewer, Antikiano = `pw_mode 'temp'`, a password in the vault): the copied text is `اسم المستخدم: antikiano / الإيميل: … / لينك التفعيل: هيوصله لما بوابة المبتكر تشتغل`; the same for ops. The maker has a temporary password held by the admin, no link is planned for him, and ops is the role most likely to hand the details over on WhatsApp. For the admin / `vendors.edit = F` the text is right (`الباسورد المؤقت: …`, spec `:259`).
*Minimal fix (block 28, decision 9 b):* in `copyLogin`, when `!(canLogin && L0.hasPassword)` choose the line by mode: `L0.pwMode === 'temp' || L0.hasPassword ? 'الباسورد المؤقت: اطلبه من الأدمن أو متابعة المبتكرين' : 'لينك التفعيل: هيوصله لما بوابة المبتكر تشتغل'` (or hide `[data-copy-login]` when `!canLogin && L0.hasPassword`); one assertion in `vendors-add.spec.mjs` for ops.
